Skyscraper Frog

8-bit leaping across the rooftops.

You and the bravest frog ever will experience a journey like no other. In this 8-bit style game, help choose the frog’s path across the roofs of the tallest skyscrapers. When the fly comes and goes, don’t miss your chance — eat it for a vital boost!

Questions about Skyscraper Frog

Does Skyscraper Frog work offline?+

Yes. Skyscraper Frog runs entirely on your device, so it works with no internet connection.

Is there an account or subscription?+

No. There's no sign-up, no login, and no subscription — just download Skyscraper Frog from the App Store and open it.

What data does Skyscraper Frog collect?+

None. It collects, stores, and transmits zero personal data — nothing ever leaves your device.

Which devices are supported?+

Skyscraper Frog is available for iPhone, iPad & Mac.
