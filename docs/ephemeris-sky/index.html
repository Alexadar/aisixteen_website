Ephemeris Sky

The sky, computed for any moment.

Turn any moment into a precise picture of the solar system. Ephemeris Sky computes real geocentric planetary positions, the aspects between them, and a live chart wheel for any date and time. Save your natal chart and read today’s sky against it, compare two charts with synastry that ranks every contact, score dignities and see the balance of a chart, and get the next solar or lunar return to the minute. Choose your house system — Placidus, Koch, Whole Sign and more — and follow an event timeline of ingresses, lunations and aspects.

Questions about Ephemeris Sky

Which house systems does Ephemeris Sky support?+

Six: Placidus, Koch, Whole Sign, Equal, Campanus and Regiomontanus. The Ascendant, Midheaven, Descendant and IC are computed with all twelve cusps, drawn on the wheel and listed by sign and degree.

How accurate are the planetary positions?+

Positions are geocentric and tropical, referred to the equinox of date. They are validated against NASA/JPL Horizons across 1900–2100: better than 7 arcminutes for every body, typically under 2.

What if the birth time is unknown?+

Positions still compute. Houses and angles are left out, with an explanation, rather than calculated from an assumed noon, and Moon contacts carry the range the missing time leaves open.

Does Ephemeris Sky write horoscopes or predictions?+

No. It computes positions, aspects, houses, dignities, returns and cycle dates and shows the numbers. It does not interpret a chart or predict anything.

Does Ephemeris Sky work offline?+

Yes. Every calculation runs on your device, so it works with no internet connection. Anything you save syncs through your own iCloud whenever a connection is available.

Is there an account or subscription?+

No. There's no sign-up, no login, and no subscription — just download Ephemeris Sky from the App Store and open it.

What data does Ephemeris Sky collect?+

None. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and we receive nothing. Anything you save is stored in your own private iCloud container, not on our servers.

Which devices are supported?+

Ephemeris Sky is available for iPhone, iPad & Mac.
