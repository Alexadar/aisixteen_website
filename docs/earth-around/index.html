Questions about Earth Around

Where does Earth Around get its data?+

From public feeds: NOAA's Space Weather Prediction Center for the G, R and S scales, solar wind, Bz and GOES X-ray flux, and GFZ for the Hp30 index.

What is Hp30, and how is it different from Kp?+

Kp is the planetary geomagnetic index published for 3-hour intervals. Hp30 is a Kp-like index from GFZ with a 30-minute cadence, so a short, sharp disturbance shows up sooner than it does in Kp.

Does a high aurora chance mean I will see the aurora?+

Not by itself. Earth Around shows tonight's aurora chance and the view line; whether you see anything also depends on darkness and cloud where you stand.

Does Earth Around need an internet connection?+

Yes, for live readings. Earth Around pulls public NOAA feeds to show current conditions; previously loaded values stay visible offline.

Is there an account or subscription?+

No. There's no sign-up, no login, and no subscription — just download Earth Around from the App Store and open it.

What data does Earth Around collect?+

None. It collects no personal data and has no analytics or tracking — the only network requests are to the public data feeds it displays.

Which devices are supported?+

Earth Around is available for iPhone, iPad, Mac & Apple Watch.
