Earth Around

Watch the Sun, from your wrist.

Earth Around watches the Sun for you. See whether a storm is happening right now on the NOAA G, R and S scales, what is driving it — solar wind speed and Bz — and live GOES X-ray flux for flares. Check tonight's aurora chance and view line, and read Hp30 for 30-minute storm resolution. On iPhone, iPad, Mac, and right on your Apple Watch.

Questions

Does Earth Around need an internet connection?+

Yes, for live readings. Earth Around pulls public NOAA feeds to show current conditions; previously loaded values stay visible offline.

Is there an account or subscription?+

No. There's no sign-up, no login, and no subscription — just download Earth Around from the App Store and open it.

What data does Earth Around collect?+

None. It collects no personal data and has no analytics or tracking — the only network requests are to the public data feeds it displays.

Which devices are supported?+

Earth Around is available for iPhone, iPad, Mac & Apple Watch.
