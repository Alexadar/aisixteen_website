Overtone Lab

Twenty exact audio tools in one app.

A precision calculation studio for audio engineers, musicians, and instrument builders — twenty exact tools in one app. Compute tempo and timing, SMPTE, pitch and harmonics, string tension, room acoustics, filters and crossovers, LUFS loudness, and more. Every formula is reference-validated.

What's inside

  • Tempo, SMPTE timecode, pitch & harmonics
  • Reverb, room modes, filters & crossovers
  • ITU-R BS.1770 LUFS loudness metering

Questions about Overtone Lab

Does Overtone Lab work offline?+

Yes. Overtone Lab runs entirely on your device, so it works with no internet connection.

Is there an account or subscription?+

No. There's no sign-up, no login, and no subscription — just download Overtone Lab from the App Store and open it.

What data does Overtone Lab collect?+

None. It collects, stores, and transmits zero personal data — nothing ever leaves your device.

Which devices are supported?+

Overtone Lab is available for iPhone, iPad & Mac.
