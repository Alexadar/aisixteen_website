AirCore

The offline air-side HVAC calculator.

The air-side calculator for HVAC technicians, designers and contractors. Enter any two of dry bulb, wet bulb, dew point, relative humidity, humidity ratio, enthalpy or specific volume and AirCore solves the rest, putting the point on a real psychrometric chart — type a value and the point moves, drag the point and the values follow. Site elevation sits in the toolbar of every screen and every number is corrected to it, so you are not quietly working at sea level. Imperial and metric switch freely without re-rounding, and the physics lives in independently tested modules checked against published reference data.

Questions about AirCore

Which air properties can AirCore solve from?+

Any two of seven: dry bulb, wet bulb, dew point, relative humidity, humidity ratio, enthalpy and specific volume. The rest of the moist-air state is solved and plotted on a psychrometric chart.

What does AirCore not do?+

It computes air properties and sizes. It has no fitting library and does not do load calculations.

What is the psychrometric math checked against?+

Against CoolProp and IAPWS-95 reference values, which are independent of the relations the app implements, and against published psychrometric-table anchors.

Does AirCore work offline?+

Yes. AirCore runs entirely on your device, so it works with no internet connection.

Is there an account or subscription?+

No. There's no sign-up, no login, and no subscription — just download AirCore from the App Store and open it.

What data does AirCore collect?+

None. It collects, stores, and transmits zero personal data — nothing ever leaves your device.

Which devices are supported?+

AirCore is available for iPhone, iPad, Mac & Apple Watch.
