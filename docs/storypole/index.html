Storypole

Layout, lumber and takeoff, on site.

A job-site calculator for carpenters, framers and remodelers. Run feet-inch-fraction tape math, lay out equal spacing and on-center marks, size lumber in board feet with nominal-versus-dressed dimensions, work roof pitch and rafters, square up and solve miters and bevels, take off concrete in cubic yards, and check wire gauge. Everything runs on your device — including on your wrist, right at the wall.

Questions about Storypole

Does Storypole measure with the camera?+

No. There is no camera and no AR ruler. Storypole does the math on a measurement you already took with a real tape.

Which on-center spacings does the layout tool mark?+

16", 19.2" and 24", with the odd last bay stated. Equal spacing gives the full list of marks to pull, not just a count.

Where do the reference values come from?+

The Reference tab names the standard behind each sourced value, including NIST PS 20-20 for dressed lumber sizes and NBS Handbook 100 for wire gauge. Values with no published source are labelled as derived: 19.2" on center is 96" ÷ 5.

Does Storypole work offline?+

Yes. Storypole runs entirely on your device, so it works with no internet connection.

Is there an account or subscription?+

No. There's no sign-up, no login, and no subscription — just download Storypole from the App Store and open it.

What data does Storypole collect?+

None. It collects, stores, and transmits zero personal data — nothing ever leaves your device.

Which devices are supported?+

Storypole is available for iPhone, iPad, Mac & Apple Watch.
